## About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at the Federal University of Santa Maria (UFSM), in Santa Maria, Brazil, focused on **backend development with Python**: Django, FastAPI and Flask, REST APIs, and relational databases. Most of what I build is software that other people depend on: research tooling, data pipelines and web systems used inside the university. Lately that means public-health software at PET-Saúde and generative models for network traffic at GRECA.
